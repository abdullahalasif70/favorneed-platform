FAQ — সাধারণ প্রশ্ন

Common Questions

How does Favorneed verify campaigns?

ফেভারনিড কীভাবে ক্যাম্পেইন যাচাই করে?
+
Every campaign goes through NID verification, document review, and for medical cases, an independent doctor confirmation. Our admin panel reviews each case before it goes live.

What payment methods are supported?

কোন পেমেন্ট পদ্ধতি সমর্থিত?
+
We support bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and all debit/credit cards through ShurjoPay — Bangladesh's leading payment gateway.

What if a campaign turns out to be fraudulent?

ক্যাম্পেইন জালিয়াতি হলে কী হবে?
+
If fraud is proven through our investigation process, every donor receives a 100% refund. This is our core guarantee and non-negotiable commitment.

Can I donate anonymously?

আমি কি বেনামে দান করতে পারি?
+
Yes. Donors can choose to give publicly with their name shown, or completely anonymously — the choice is always yours.
